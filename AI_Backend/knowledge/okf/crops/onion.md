# Onion

Stop water before lifting, or the crop will not store.

Type: Vegetable (bulb). Season: Rabi and kharif.

## Nutrition

- Sulphur affects pungency and storage life; it is often the missing nutrient.

## Soil

- Well-drained loam. A shallow root system means it cannot chase water deep.

## Problems to watch

- Thrips in dry weather - check leaf axils, where they hide.
- Purple blotch in humid spells.

## Harvest

- Lift when about half the tops have fallen, then cure in shade with airflow before storage.

## Do and do not

The rules the task scheduler attaches to this crop's jobs.

### Irrigation

- Do not: Stop irrigation 15-20 days before harvest - bulbs lifted wet do not store.
