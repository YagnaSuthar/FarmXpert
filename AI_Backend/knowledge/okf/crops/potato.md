# Potato

Steady moisture during bulking, and a killed haulm before lifting.

Type: Tuber. Season: Rabi (October-November planting).

- Salt tolerance is ECe, measured on a saturated paste extract in a laboratory (FAO-29, Maas-Hoffman). A field meter reading a 1:2 soil-water sample gives a different, lower number - the two must not be compared directly.
- The depletion fraction p is the share of the soil's available water this crop can use before it starts to suffer. At p = 0.35, irrigate once about 35 percent of the available water in the root zone has been used.

## Water

- Keep moisture steady through tuber bulking. A dry spell followed by heavy watering makes the tuber grow in a burst, which is what causes hollow heart and growth cracks.
- Stop irrigation before lifting so the soil is friable and the skin sets.

## Nutrition

- Potassium is heavily used. Excess nitrogen delays bulking and grows haulm.

## Soil

- Loose, well-drained loam. Clods at planting mean misshapen tubers.

## Problems to watch

- Do not plant into cold wet soil; seed pieces rot before they sprout.

## Harvest

- Cure in shade before storage; tubers stored warm and damp rot.

## Do and do not

The rules the task scheduler attaches to this crop's jobs.

### Harvesting

- Do: Kill the haulm 10-15 days before lifting so the skin sets and the tubers do not scuff.

### Irrigation

- Do not: Do not let the soil swing between dry and wet during tuber bulking - that is what causes hollow heart and cracking.

### Pest control

- Do: Watch for late blight whenever nights are cool and leaves stay wet; a preventive spray beats a curative one.

## Soil notes

- Keep pH below 6.5 to limit common scab.
- High potassium demand for tuber bulking; sulphate of potash improves tuber quality.
