# Maize

Tasselling and silking are the two weeks that decide the cob.

Type: Cereal. Season: Kharif and rabi.

- Salt tolerance is ECe, measured on a saturated paste extract in a laboratory (FAO-29, Maas-Hoffman). A field meter reading a 1:2 soil-water sample gives a different, lower number - the two must not be compared directly.
- The depletion fraction p is the share of the soil's available water this crop can use before it starts to suffer. At p = 0.55, irrigate once about 55 percent of the available water in the root zone has been used.

## Nutrition

- A heavy nitrogen feeder. Split the dose and place it beside the row, not against the stem.

## Soil

- Well-drained loam. It is sensitive to waterlogging at the seedling stage.

## Problems to watch

- Fall armyworm: scout whorls early; an infestation found late is far harder to treat.
- Stalk rot follows water stress at grain filling.

## Harvest

- Harvest when the black layer forms at the kernel base and husks are dry.

## Do and do not

The rules the task scheduler attaches to this crop's jobs.

### Irrigation

- Do: Tasselling and silking are the critical stages - stress for even a few days there cuts the cob directly.

## Soil notes

- Heavy nitrogen feeder: split N at sowing, knee-high and tasselling.
- Zinc deficiency (white bud) on calcareous soils - zinc sulphate 25 kg/ha.
