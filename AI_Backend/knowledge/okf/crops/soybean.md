# Soybean

Inoculate the seed and keep nitrogen low; pod filling is the stage to protect.

Type: Oilseed legume. Season: Kharif (June-July sowing).

- Salt tolerance is ECe, measured on a saturated paste extract in a laboratory (FAO-29, Maas-Hoffman). A field meter reading a 1:2 soil-water sample gives a different, lower number - the two must not be compared directly.
- The depletion fraction p is the share of the soil's available water this crop can use before it starts to suffer. At p = 0.5, irrigate once about 50 percent of the available water in the root zone has been used.

## Water

- Flowering and pod filling are critical. Waterlogging at either is as damaging as drought.

## Soil

- Well-drained loam; it will not tolerate standing water for long.

## Problems to watch

- Girdle beetle and semilooper; scout weekly through vegetative growth.
- Yellow mosaic virus spreads by whitefly - control the vector, not the symptom.

## Harvest

- Harvest as soon as pods rattle; delay and the pods shatter in the field.

## Do and do not

The rules the task scheduler attaches to this crop's jobs.

### Fertilization

- Do: Inoculate the seed with rhizobium; it replaces most of the nitrogen dose.
- Do not: Do not apply heavy nitrogen - it suppresses the nodules that fix it for free.

## Soil notes

- Inoculate seed with Bradyrhizobium; nodulation needs pH 6.0-7.0.
- Sulphur 20-30 kg/ha improves oil and protein.
