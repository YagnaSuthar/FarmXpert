# Cabbage

Head formation is the critical stage; uneven watering splits heads.

Type: Vegetable (brassica). Season: Rabi (September-November transplanting).

- Salt tolerance is ECe, measured on a saturated paste extract in a laboratory (FAO-29, Maas-Hoffman). A field meter reading a 1:2 soil-water sample gives a different, lower number - the two must not be compared directly.
- The depletion fraction p is the share of the soil's available water this crop can use before it starts to suffer. At p = 0.45, irrigate once about 45 percent of the available water in the root zone has been used.

## Soil

- Fertile, well-drained loam with steady moisture; it is a shallow-rooted, hungry crop.

## Problems to watch

- Diamondback moth resists many products - rotate chemical groups and use biological options early.
- Aphids inside a forming head cannot be reached by a spray; act before heading.

## Harvest

- Cut when heads are firm. A head left past maturity splits after the next irrigation or rain.

## Do and do not

The rules the task scheduler attaches to this crop's jobs.

### Irrigation

- Do not: Do not swing between dry and heavy watering during head formation - the heads split.

## Soil notes

- Boron deficiency causes hollow stems, and molybdenum deficiency causes whiptail on acid soils - lime acid soils and apply borax 10 kg/ha.
- Club root thrives in acid soil; liming to pH 7 suppresses it.
