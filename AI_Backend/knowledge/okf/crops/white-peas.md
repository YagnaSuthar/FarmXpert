# White peas

A cool-season pulse: sow on time, and keep the pod-fill stage watered.

Type: Pulse legume. Season: Rabi (October-November sowing).

- Salt tolerance is ECe, measured on a saturated paste extract in a laboratory (FAO-29, Maas-Hoffman). A field meter reading a 1:2 soil-water sample gives a different, lower number - the two must not be compared directly.
- The depletion fraction p is the share of the soil's available water this crop can use before it starts to suffer. At p = 0.4, irrigate once about 40 percent of the available water in the root zone has been used.

## Nutrition

- A legume - inoculate the seed; a small starter dose of phosphorus helps early growth.

## Soil

- Well-drained loam, near neutral pH. Poor drainage brings root rot.

## Problems to watch

- Powdery mildew late in the season, and pod borer at pod fill.
- Sowing late pushes pod fill into heat, which shrivels the grain.

## Do and do not

The rules the task scheduler attaches to this crop's jobs.

### Harvesting

- Do: Harvest when pods are dry and firm; over-dry pods shatter during threshing.

### Irrigation

- Do: Protect flowering and pod filling; the rest of the season needs very little.
- Do not: Do not let water stand at any stage - white peas do not tolerate waterlogging.

## Soil notes

- Inoculate with Rhizobium; a 20 kg N/ha starter is enough.
