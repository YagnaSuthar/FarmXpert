# Banana

Shallow roots and a large leaf area: it needs water almost continuously.

Type: Perennial fruit. Season: Perennial; planted June-July or February-March.

## Water

- Drip is strongly preferred; flooding wastes water and spreads disease.

## Nutrition

- A heavy potassium feeder. Split doses through the season.

## Soil

- Deep, well-drained loam with high organic matter. Waterlogging kills the mat.

## Problems to watch

- Panama wilt and sigatoka; remove and destroy affected material rather than spraying around it.
- Prop the bunch: a wind-thrown plant at bunch stage is a total loss for that plant.

## Harvest

- Harvest at three-quarter fullness for distant markets.
